Ethics in service

Everyday ways to honour trusts, act fairly, and preserve the dignity of colleagues.

OverviewWorship during serviceEthics in serviceZad for new recruitsFor personnel on the southern border

Honour what you are entrusted with

Return what was entrusted to you to its owner, and be clear about what you know when passing on information. The Qur’an pairs honouring trusts with judging fairly between people.

Qur’an 4:58

Treat people fairly

Assess what people say and do fairly. Personal disagreements should never lead to unfair treatment. The verse calls for justice even when there is hostility.

Qur’an 5:8

Respect privacy and speak carefully

Avoid backbiting and spying. When seeking advice on a general question, describe the issue without posting people’s names or personal information, or the unit’s details or location.

Qur’an 49:12Sahih al-Bukhari 6018
