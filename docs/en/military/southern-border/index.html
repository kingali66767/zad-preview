For personnel on the southern border

Religious references on prayer in a state of fear, travel and purification for personnel serving on the southern border.

Prayer in a state of fear

The verse in Al-Baqarah establishes a concession in the manner of prayer in circumstances of genuine fear and calls for ordinary worship to resume when safe. Seek qualified religious guidance about applying this to a specific situation.

Qur’an 2:239Qur’an 4:103Al-Sa’di’s commentary in Arabic

Combining and shortening prayers

Shortening reduces the four-unit prayers of Dhuhr, Asr and Isha to two units. Combining means performing Dhuhr with Asr or Maghrib with Isha in one of their appointed times when there is a valid reason. The two have separate rulings.

Ibn Baz on combining and shortening

Travel, residence and shortening prayer

Shortening is tied to traveler status. Ibn Baz distinguishes a planned stay of a known duration from staying until a need is met without a known end date. He also notes disagreement on some cases. Include your intended length of stay when asking for guidance.

Dry ablution when water cannot be used

Dry ablution is a concession when water cannot be obtained or used for a valid reason. Read the verse and seek guidance if you are unsure whether the concession applies to your situation.

Qur’an 5:6Ibn Baz on when dry ablution is permittedAblution and prayer guide

Wiping over leather socks or socks

Among the conditions for wiping are putting on the footwear after purification and covering the area required to be washed. Ibn Baz explains the time limits for residents and travelers and the other conditions.

Ibn Baz on wiping over footwear

Prayer times during shifts

Prayer has appointed times. Use the city timetable to plan around them. If a particular situation prevents you from praying in the ordinary manner, ask a religious adviser about your case.

Qur’an 4:103Prayer times for Saudi cities

How to ask about your situation

For personal rulings, consult a religious adviser or a qualified fatwa authority. In public search, describe the relevant circumstance, such as travel or a stay, without names or details about your unit or duty location.
