Remembrance in distress

Supplications and remembrance in times of hardship, from the Quran and Sahih al-Bukhari.

The supplication for distress narrated by Ibn Abbas

لَا إِلَهَ إِلَّا اللَّهُ الْعَظِيمُ الْحَلِيمُ، لَا إِلَهَ إِلَّا اللَّهُ رَبُّ الْعَرْشِ الْعَظِيمِ، لَا إِلَهَ إِلَّا اللَّهُ رَبُّ السَّمَاوَاتِ وَرَبُّ الْأَرْضِ وَرَبُّ الْعَرْشِ الْكَرِيمِ.

Meaning None is worthy of worship but Allah, the Magnificent, the Forbearing. None is worthy of worship but Allah, Lord of the Mighty Throne. None is worthy of worship but Allah, Lord of the heavens and the earth and Lord of the Noble Throne.

Ibn Abbas narrated that the Prophet said this when in distress.

Sahih al-Bukhari 6346

The supplication of Jonah, peace be upon him

لَا إِلَهَ إِلَّا أَنْتَ سُبْحَانَكَ إِنِّي كُنْتُ مِنَ الظَّالِمِينَ.

Meaning None is worthy of worship but You. Glory be to You. I have been among the wrongdoers.

Jonah made this supplication in the darkness. The next verse tells how Allah answered him and saved him from distress.

The Qur’an 21:87-88
