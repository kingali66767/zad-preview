Remembrance at home

Remember Allah and greet with peace when you enter, and place your trust in Him as you leave.

Remembering Allah on entering

Remember Allah when you enter your home. Jabir’s hadith explains the effect of remembering Allah when entering the home and when eating.

Sahih Muslim 2018a

Greeting others with peace on entering

فَإِذَا دَخَلْتُمْ بُيُوتًا فَسَلِّمُوا عَلَىٰ أَنْفُسِكُمْ تَحِيَّةً مِنْ عِنْدِ اللَّهِ مُبَارَكَةً طَيِّبَةً.

Meaning When you enter houses, greet one another with peace, a blessed and good greeting from Allah.

Greet with peace when you enter. The verse in Surah al-Nur describes this greeting as blessed and good.

The Qur’an 24:61

Trusting Allah when leaving home

بِسْمِ اللَّهِ، تَوَكَّلْتُ عَلَى اللَّهِ، لَا حَوْلَ وَلَا قُوَّةَ إِلَّا بِاللَّهِ.

Meaning In the name of Allah. I place my trust in Allah. There is no power and no strength except through Allah.

Say this when leaving home, as narrated by Anas ibn Malik.

Sunan Abi Dawud 5095
