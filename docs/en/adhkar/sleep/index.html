Bedtime remembrances

Remembrances and recitations for bedtime, including the last two verses of al-Baqarah for the night.

Al-Ikhlas, al-Falaq, and al-Nas

3 times

Cup your hands together and blow lightly into them, recite the three surahs, then wipe as much of your body as you can with your hands. Begin with the head, face, and front of the body. Repeat the entire sequence three times.

Sahih al-Bukhari 5017 The Qur’an 112 The Qur’an 113 The Qur’an 114

Supplication at bedtime

بِاسْمِكَ اللَّهُمَّ أَمُوتُ وَأَحْيَا.

Meaning In Your name, O Allah, I die and I live.

The Prophet said this when he went to bed, as narrated by Hudhayfah.

Sahih al-Bukhari 6324

The Throne Verse

Recite the whole Throne Verse when you go to bed. The Prophet confirmed what Abu Hurayrah learned in the account of guarding the Ramadan charity.

The Qur’an 2:255 Sahih al-Bukhari 2311

Fatimah’s bedtime remembrance

33 glorifications · 33 praises · 34 declarations of Allah’s greatness

When going to bed, say “Glory be to Allah” thirty-three times, “Praise belongs to Allah” thirty-three times, and “Allah is the Greatest” thirty-four times. The Prophet taught this remembrance to Ali and Fatimah.

Sahih al-Bukhari 5361

The last two verses of al-Baqarah at night

Recite the last two verses of al-Baqarah at night. The hadith says they suffice for whoever recites them that night.

The Qur’an 2:285-286 Sahih al-Bukhari 5009
