Mealtime remembrances

Begin eating in Allah’s name and praise Him when you finish.

Saying Allah’s name before eating

بِسْمِ اللَّهِ.

Meaning In the name of Allah.

Mention Allah’s name before you begin eating. The Prophet taught Umar ibn Abi Salamah to mention Allah’s name, eat with his right hand, and eat from what was nearest to him.

Sahih al-Bukhari 5376 Sahih Muslim 2018a

Praising Allah when the meal is cleared

الْحَمْدُ لِلَّهِ كَثِيرًا طَيِّبًا مُبَارَكًا فِيهِ، غَيْرَ مَكْفِيٍّ، وَلَا مُوَدَّعٍ وَلَا مُسْتَغْنًى عَنْهُ، رَبَّنَا.

Meaning All praise belongs to Allah, abundant, good, and blessed. We can never praise Him enough, bid Him farewell, or do without Him, our Lord.

This praise after eating appears in the hadith of Abu Umamah in Sahih al-Bukhari.

Sahih al-Bukhari 5458
