Remembrance on waking

Praise Allah when you wake. There is also a remembrance for waking during the night.

Praising Allah on waking

الْحَمْدُ لِلَّهِ الَّذِي أَحْيَانَا بَعْدَ مَا أَمَاتَنَا، وَإِلَيْهِ النُّشُورُ.

Meaning All praise belongs to Allah, who brought us to life after causing us to die, and to Him is the resurrection.

This remembrance on waking is narrated by Hudhayfah.

Sahih al-Bukhari 6324

Waking during the night

لَا إِلَهَ إِلَّا اللَّهُ وَحْدَهُ لَا شَرِيكَ لَهُ، لَهُ الْمُلْكُ وَلَهُ الْحَمْدُ، وَهُوَ عَلَى كُلِّ شَيْءٍ قَدِيرٌ.

Meaning None is worthy of worship but Allah alone, without partner. His is the dominion and the praise, and He has power over all things.

Say this declaration of Allah’s oneness. Then praise Allah, glorify Him, declare His oneness, say that He is the Greatest, and say “There is no power and no strength except through Allah,” in the narrated order. Then ask forgiveness or make a supplication for what you need. Read the full wording in the hadith of Ubadah ibn al-Samit.

Sahih al-Bukhari 1154
