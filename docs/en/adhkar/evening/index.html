Evening remembrances

Supplications and recitations for the evening.

We have entered the evening

أَمْسَيْنَا وَأَمْسَى الْمُلْكُ لِلَّهِ، وَالْحَمْدُ لِلَّهِ، لَا إِلَهَ إِلَّا اللَّهُ وَحْدَهُ لَا شَرِيكَ لَهُ، لَهُ الْمُلْكُ وَلَهُ الْحَمْدُ وَهُوَ عَلَى كُلِّ شَيْءٍ قَدِيرٌ. رَبِّ أَسْأَلُكَ خَيْرَ مَا فِي هَذِهِ اللَّيْلَةِ وَخَيْرَ مَا بَعْدَهَا، وَأَعُوذُ بِكَ مِنْ شَرِّ مَا فِي هَذِهِ اللَّيْلَةِ وَشَرِّ مَا بَعْدَهَا. رَبِّ أَعُوذُ بِكَ مِنَ الْكَسَلِ وَسُوءِ الْكِبَرِ، رَبِّ أَعُوذُ بِكَ مِنْ عَذَابٍ فِي النَّارِ وَعَذَابٍ فِي الْقَبْرِ.

Meaning We have entered the evening, and all dominion belongs to Allah. All praise belongs to Allah. None is worthy of worship but Allah alone, without partner. His is the dominion and the praise, and He has power over all things. My Lord, I ask You for the good of this night and what follows it, and I seek refuge in You from the evil of this night and what follows it. My Lord, I seek refuge in You from laziness and the ills of old age. My Lord, I seek refuge in You from punishment in the Fire and in the grave.

This evening supplication in the hadith of Abdullah ibn Mas‘ud asks for the good of the night and seeks refuge from its evil.

Sahih Muslim 2723b

O Allah, by You we enter the evening

اللَّهُمَّ بِكَ أَمْسَيْنَا، وَبِكَ نَحْيَا، وَبِكَ نَمُوتُ، وَإِلَيْكَ النُّشُورُ.

Meaning O Allah, by You we enter the evening, by You we live and by You we die, and to You is the resurrection.

The evening wording narrated by Abu Dawud from Abu Hurayrah.

Sunan Abi Dawud 5068

Al-Ikhlas, al-Falaq, and al-Nas

3 times for each surah

قُلْ هُوَ اللَّهُ أَحَدٌ ۝ اللَّهُ الصَّمَدُ ۝ لَمْ يَلِدْ وَلَمْ يُولَدْ ۝ وَلَمْ يَكُنْ لَهُ كُفُوًا أَحَدٌ
قُلْ أَعُوذُ بِرَبِّ الْفَلَقِ ۝ مِنْ شَرِّ مَا خَلَقَ ۝ وَمِنْ شَرِّ غَاسِقٍ إِذَا وَقَبَ ۝ وَمِنْ شَرِّ النَّفَّاثَاتِ فِي الْعُقَدِ ۝ وَمِنْ شَرِّ حَاسِدٍ إِذَا حَسَدَ
قُلْ أَعُوذُ بِرَبِّ النَّاسِ ۝ مَلِكِ النَّاسِ ۝ إِلَهِ النَّاسِ ۝ مِنْ شَرِّ الْوَسْوَاسِ الْخَنَّاسِ ۝ الَّذِي يُوَسْوِسُ فِي صُدُورِ النَّاسِ ۝ مِنَ الْجِنَّةِ وَالنَّاسِ

Meaning Al-Ikhlas affirms that Allah is One, the One on whom all depend. He neither begets nor was born, and none is equal to Him. Al-Falaq asks the Lord of daybreak for protection from the evil of what He created, the darkness when it settles, those who blow on knots, and an envier when they envy. Al-Nas asks the Lord, King, and God of humankind for protection from the retreating whisperer who whispers into people’s hearts, whether from among jinn or humankind.

Recite the three surahs in the morning and evening. Abdullah ibn Khubayb’s hadith gives the number of repetitions.

Sunan Abi Dawud 5082 The Qur’an 112 The Qur’an 113 The Qur’an 114

The foremost prayer for forgiveness

اللَّهُمَّ أَنْتَ رَبِّي لَا إِلَهَ إِلَّا أَنْتَ، خَلَقْتَنِي وَأَنَا عَبْدُكَ، وَأَنَا عَلَى عَهْدِكَ وَوَعْدِكَ مَا اسْتَطَعْتُ، أَعُوذُ بِكَ مِنْ شَرِّ مَا صَنَعْتُ، أَبُوءُ لَكَ بِنِعْمَتِكَ عَلَيَّ، وَأَبُوءُ لَكَ بِذَنْبِي، فَاغْفِرْ لِي، فَإِنَّهُ لَا يَغْفِرُ الذُّنُوبَ إِلَّا أَنْتَ.

Meaning O Allah, You are my Lord. None is worthy of worship but You. You created me and I am Your servant. I hold to Your covenant and promise as far as I can. I seek refuge in You from the evil I have done. I acknowledge Your blessings upon me and I acknowledge my sin. Forgive me, for none forgives sins but You.

Shaddad ibn Aws’s hadith describes the merit of saying this with conviction during the day or at night.

Sahih al-Bukhari 6306

In Allah’s name, with whose name nothing can harm

3 times

بِسْمِ اللَّهِ الَّذِي لَا يَضُرُّ مَعَ اسْمِهِ شَيْءٌ فِي الْأَرْضِ وَلَا فِي السَّمَاءِ، وَهُوَ السَّمِيعُ الْعَلِيمُ.

Meaning In the name of Allah, with whose name nothing on earth or in heaven can harm. He is the All-Hearing, the All-Knowing.

Say this three times each morning and evening, as narrated by Uthman ibn Affan.

Jami‘ at-Tirmidhi 3388

Asking for pardon and well-being

اللَّهُمَّ إِنِّي أَسْأَلُكَ الْعَافِيَةَ فِي الدُّنْيَا وَالْآخِرَةِ، اللَّهُمَّ إِنِّي أَسْأَلُكَ الْعَفْوَ وَالْعَافِيَةَ فِي دِينِي وَدُنْيَايَ وَأَهْلِي وَمَالِي، اللَّهُمَّ اسْتُرْ عَوْرَاتِي وَآمِنْ رَوْعَاتِي، اللَّهُمَّ احْفَظْنِي مِنْ بَيْنِ يَدَيَّ وَمِنْ خَلْفِي وَعَنْ يَمِينِي وَعَنْ شِمَالِي وَمِنْ فَوْقِي، وَأَعُوذُ بِعَظَمَتِكَ أَنْ أُغْتَالَ مِنْ تَحْتِي.

Meaning O Allah, I ask You for well-being in this life and the Hereafter. I ask You for pardon and well-being in my faith, my worldly life, my family, and my wealth. Conceal my vulnerabilities and calm my fears. Protect me from ahead, behind, to my right, to my left, and above me. I seek refuge in Your greatness from being struck from below.

Abdullah ibn Umar reported that the Prophet regularly said this supplication in the morning and evening.

Sunan Abi Dawud 5074

Seeking refuge from the evil of the self and Satan

اللَّهُمَّ فَاطِرَ السَّمَاوَاتِ وَالْأَرْضِ، عَالِمَ الْغَيْبِ وَالشَّهَادَةِ، رَبَّ كُلِّ شَيْءٍ وَمَلِيكَهُ، أَشْهَدُ أَنْ لَا إِلَهَ إِلَّا أَنْتَ، أَعُوذُ بِكَ مِنْ شَرِّ نَفْسِي وَشَرِّ الشَّيْطَانِ وَشِرْكِهِ.

Meaning O Allah, Creator of the heavens and the earth, Knower of the unseen and the seen, Lord and Sovereign of all things, I testify that none is worthy of worship but You. I seek refuge in You from the evil of my own self, the evil of Satan, and the association of partners with You to which he calls.

The Prophet taught Abu Bakr al-Siddiq to say this in the morning, in the evening, and when going to bed.

Sunan Abi Dawud 5067

Seeking refuge in the perfect words of Allah

3 times

أَعُوذُ بِكَلِمَاتِ اللَّهِ التَّامَّاتِ مِنْ شَرِّ مَا خَلَقَ.

Meaning I seek refuge in the perfect words of Allah from the evil of what He created.

Say this in the evening. The remembrance is narrated in Sahih Muslim. The three repetitions are given in al-Tirmidhi’s narration, which al-Albani graded authentic.

Sahih Muslim 2709a Dorar hadith encyclopedia

Glorifying and praising Allah

100 times

سُبْحَانَ اللَّهِ وَبِحَمْدِهِ.

Meaning Glory and praise be to Allah.

Abu Hurayrah’s hadith records saying this one hundred times in the morning and one hundred times in the evening.
