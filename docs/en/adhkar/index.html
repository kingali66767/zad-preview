Daily remembrance

Choose a time or occasion to read the remembrance, its reported repetition count and its source.

Morning remembrancesSupplications and recitations for the morning. Evening remembrancesSupplications and recitations for the evening. Remembrance after prayerSay these remembrances after ending an obligatory prayer with the greeting of peace. Choose one of the narrated forms of glorification. Bedtime remembrancesRemembrances and recitations for bedtime, including the last two verses of al-Baqarah for the night. Remembrance on wakingPraise Allah when you wake. There is also a remembrance for waking during the night. Remembrance at homeRemember Allah and greet with peace when you enter, and place your trust in Him as you leave. Mealtime remembrancesBegin eating in Allah’s name and praise Him when you finish. Everyday remembrancesWhat to say when entering or leaving the toilet, and when someone sneezes and praises Allah. Mosque and call to prayerWhat to say when entering and leaving the mosque, and the supplication to say after hearing the call to prayer. Travel remembrancesWhat to say when setting out, during the journey, and on returning. Remembrance in distressSupplications and remembrance in times of hardship, from the Quran and Sahih al-Bukhari. General remembrancesGlorify Allah, acknowledge that all strength comes through Him, and seek His forgiveness throughout the day.
