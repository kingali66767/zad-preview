All remembrances

Remembrance after prayer

Say these remembrances after ending an obligatory prayer with the greeting of peace. Choose one of the narrated forms of glorification.

Seeking forgiveness, then “O Allah, You are Peace”

Seek forgiveness 3 times

اللَّهُمَّ أَنْتَ السَّلَامُ وَمِنْكَ السَّلَامُ، تَبَارَكْتَ ذَا الْجَلَالِ وَالْإِكْرَامِ.

Meaning O Allah, You are Peace and from You comes peace. Blessed are You, Possessor of Majesty and Honour.

After the closing greeting, say “I seek Allah’s forgiveness” three times, then say this supplication, as narrated by Thawban.

Sahih Muslim 591

Glorifying Allah, praising Him, and declaring His greatness and oneness

33 glorifications · 33 praises · 33 declarations of Allah’s greatness

لا إله إلا الله وحده لا شريك له، له الملك وله الحمد، وهو على كل شيء قدير.

Meaning None is worthy of worship but Allah alone, without partner. His is the dominion and the praise, and He has power over all things.

Say “Glory be to Allah,” “Praise belongs to Allah,” and “Allah is the Greatest” thirty-three times each. Complete the hundred with this declaration.

Sahih Muslim 597a

Another form of glorification and praise

33 glorifications · 33 praises · 34 declarations of Allah’s greatness

In Ka‘b ibn Ujrah’s hadith, after each obligatory prayer say “Glory be to Allah” thirty-three times, “Praise belongs to Allah” thirty-three times, and “Allah is the Greatest” thirty-four times.

Sahih Muslim 596a

O Allah, none can withhold what You give

اللَّهُمَّ لَا مَانِعَ لِمَا أَعْطَيْتَ، وَلَا مُعْطِيَ لِمَا مَنَعْتَ، وَلَا يَنْفَعُ ذَا الْجَدِّ مِنْكَ الْجَدُّ.

Meaning O Allah, none can withhold what You give, and none can give what You withhold. No one’s wealth or standing can avail them against You.

The hadith of al-Mughirah ibn Shu‘bah gives this supplication after the declaration of Allah’s oneness following obligatory prayer.

Sahih al-Bukhari 844
