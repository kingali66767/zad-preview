Travel remembrances

What to say when setting out, during the journey, and on returning.

When setting out on a journey

Say “Allah is the Greatest” 3 times

سُبْحَانَ الَّذِي سَخَّرَ لَنَا هَذَا وَمَا كُنَّا لَهُ مُقْرِنِينَ، وَإِنَّا إِلَىٰ رَبِّنَا لَمُنْقَلِبُونَ. اللَّهُمَّ إِنَّا نَسْأَلُكَ فِي سَفَرِنَا هَذَا الْبِرَّ وَالتَّقْوَى، وَمِنَ الْعَمَلِ مَا تَرْضَى. اللَّهُمَّ هَوِّنْ عَلَيْنَا سَفَرَنَا هَذَا وَاطْوِ عَنَّا بُعْدَهُ. اللَّهُمَّ أَنْتَ الصَّاحِبُ فِي السَّفَرِ، وَالْخَلِيفَةُ فِي الْأَهْلِ. اللَّهُمَّ إِنِّي أَعُوذُ بِكَ مِنْ وَعْثَاءِ السَّفَرِ، وَكَآبَةِ الْمَنْظَرِ، وَسُوءِ الْمُنْقَلَبِ فِي الْمَالِ وَالْأَهْلِ.

Meaning Glory be to Him who made this available to us, for we could not have mastered it ourselves, and to our Lord we will return. O Allah, we ask You for righteousness, mindfulness of You, and deeds that please You on this journey. Make this journey easy for us and shorten its distance. You are our companion on the journey and guardian of the family we leave behind. I seek refuge in You from the hardships of travel, distressing sights, and returning to hardship concerning wealth and family.

Say “Allah is the Greatest” three times when you mount or board for travel, then say this supplication, as narrated by Abdullah ibn Umar.

The Qur’an 43:13-14 Sahih Muslim 1342

When going uphill or downhill

Say “Allah is the Greatest” when ascending and “Glory be to Allah” when descending, as Jabir reported the Companions did.

Sahih al-Bukhari 2993

When returning from a journey

آيِبُونَ تَائِبُونَ عَابِدُونَ لِرَبِّنَا حَامِدُونَ.

Meaning We return, repent, worship, and praise our Lord.

The Prophet also said the travel supplication when returning and added these words.

Sahih Muslim 1342
